FAQ
When should I take ORGO?

The label suggests about 30–60 minutes before you train or within 60 minutes after. For an everyday routine, 2 pouches a day. Taking it consistently matters more than exact timing. Shake well and drink plenty of water.

What kind of protein is in it?

4 g of fish (tilapia) collagen peptides per pouch, paired with 20 mg of vitamin C. Collagen is great for connective tissue, but don't forget to keep eating protein-rich meals too.

How much creatine is in a pouch?

300 mg per pouch, 600 mg on a two-pouch day. Most creatine studies use 3–5 g a day.

Will one pouch hydrate me?

ORGO adds sodium, potassium and magnesium; pair it with water before, during and after training.

The research

The ingredients in ORGO have been studied on their own; ORGO's finished formula has not been through its own clinical trial. See the studies on our Science page.

Does it have caffeine or sugar?

Neither. ORGO has 0 mg caffeine and 0 g sugar. It's sweetened with erythritol and sucralose and made with real fruit juice.

Is ORGO right for everyone?

ORGO is formulated for athletes and active individuals: anyone who trains, competes or stays on the move and wants an easy boost on top of their regular meals. It's a supplement, not a meal replacement. Check with a healthcare professional before use if you are pregnant, nursing, taking medication, managing a medical condition, or underage/a minor. Contains fish (tilapia). Keep out of reach of children.
